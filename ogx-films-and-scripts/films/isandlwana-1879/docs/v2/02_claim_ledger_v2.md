| **S3** | War Office Intelligence Branch, *Narrative of the Field Operations Connected with the Zulu War of 1879* (London, 1881) | `zuluwar1879` | The official digest of "statements of Zulus present": the army's strength, the moon, the reserve, the captures, Zulu losses, Melvill's watch (pp. 89–90) |

### How to treat these sources (this goes on screen where it matters)
1. **Every account passes through British hands.** Mitford translated through an interpreter. The prisoner's statement was taken in captivity. Uguku's account was printed in a British service paper. They're the best Zulu-side record from the time, not neutral transcripts.
2. **We don't paraphrase them into the narrator's own certainty.** On screen they're attributed by name or role, source and year.
3. **Where the accounts disagree, we say so.** The clearest case is the army's size: 25,000 (S1) against 13,700 (S3).
