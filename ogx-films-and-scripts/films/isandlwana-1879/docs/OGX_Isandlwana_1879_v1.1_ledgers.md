Prepared by Claude, 22 October 2026, for the private v1.1 preview (`OGX_Isandlwana_1879_v1.1.mp4`). Timecodes are from the v1.1 export.

**Status: private preview.** Not script-locked, not QC-passed, not approved for release. v1.0 is kept unchanged.

- ✅ = consistent across sources.
- ⚠ = narrated only as framed; wants a page-level cite.
- ❌ = not narrated.

**Evidence level:** search-summary and web-page level. **No page-level book checks have been done** (Laband 1997; Knight 1995; the Stuart Archive). The books aren't reachable from this build machine, and the NAM, Talana and SAHO pages are blocked here, so I worked from search excerpts of them. Where a claim is more precise than that evidence supports, it has been narrowed or attributed (see "v1.1 change").
