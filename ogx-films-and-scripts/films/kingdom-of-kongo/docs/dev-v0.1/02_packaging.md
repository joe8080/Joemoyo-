## 1. Viewer question, promise and provisional answer
- **Question:** Why did Kongo's king complain to Portugal about the slave trade, and what do his letters reveal about his kingdom?
- **Promise (provisional):** You'll understand the society behind the letters, what its ruler was trying to control, and what the evidence lets us conclude.
- **Provisional answer:** Afonso wasn't calling for an end to slavery. He was protesting because free people, including his nobles and his own relatives, were being kidnapped and sold, and because Portuguese goods were letting his vassals slip out of his control. In July 1526 he asked for no slave trade at all. By October he had made a law that put every sale under the inspection of three of his officials. The letters show a kingdom with its own laws, officials and courts, and a ruler who tried to keep the trade in his own hands.
