# Kongo v0.1: scene plan and asset register
Prepared by Claude, 2 November 2026. Private.

**Look:** black, gold and sepia.
- **Real documents and objects carry the evidence.** Maps are sourced. Reconstructions are labelled every time.
- **Never:**
  - modern Kinshasa shown as Mbanza Kongo;
  - colonial-era photographs used as 16th-century evidence;
  - a generated manuscript;
  - AI faces of named people;
  - stereotyped "tribal" imagery.

**Timing:** estimated at about 135 words a minute. Joe's recording sets the real timing.

## 3. Sound plan
1. **Narration leads.** Joe's recording is the master track; music and SFX are cut to it.
2. **Music:** one restrained, licensed bed (low strings or piano, or a Central African instrument only if it's licensed and correctly attributed). **No generic "tribal Africa" drums or chants.**
3. **Silence:** no music under the kidnapping and branding lines (S21–S22), and 2 seconds of silence after "the people who were taken" (S27).
4. **SFX:** sparse and diegetic only (paper, quill, wind, water). No crowd screams, chains or whips.
5. **Stems:** narration, music and SFX on separate tracks. Mix to −14 LUFS.
6. **Phone-speaker check** before QC.
7. **Spend:** music licence or ElevenLabs music **only with Joe's approval**. A single cue cost about 900 ElevenLabs credits on Isandlwana.
