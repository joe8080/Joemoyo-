## 3. Pictures and rights
- **A10 Ethiopian painting (9:14):** dated **c. 1940**, not 1896 (the caption now reads ETHIOPIAN PAINTING · c. 1940). Commons and the museum page can't be reached from this build machine, so the date and credit follow MJ's S8 check.
- **A13 "two survivors" photo:** **removed** because its provenance is insecure. The two shots now use securely identified period images:
  - 9:01: The Graphic, 11 April 1896 (A07, crop), for "Europe told Adwa as a story about Italy";
  - 9:06: La Tribuna Illustrata, 27 January 1895, cover of Baratieri (A05), for "A disaster. A blunder. Generals lost in the dark."
- **A09 Le Petit Journal composite:**
  - The Amba Alagi panels (4:00 and 4:06) are **removed**, because their source issue can't be identified from here. In their place, 4:00 is a new real-terrain map zoom to Amba Alagi (Mekelle and Adwa marked), and 4:06 is the animated AI reconstruction N51.
  - The only A09 crop left is the released-prisoners panel at 8:26. Its credit now says "(released Italian prisoners)".
  - **Unresolved:** MJ cites the Library of Congress reference for that illustration (29 Nov 1896), but this machine could not reach it to match it visually to our crop. Confirm before release.
- **Battle maps (5:34–7:00), phone readability:**
  - The satellite base is darker, for contrast.

  - The three columns now draw **one after another**, and moves from earlier shots step back while the new one draws.
  - Brigade names (ALBERTONE, ARIMONDI, DABORMIDA, RESERVE) stay on screen and follow their brigades.
  - A **legend** (Italian-led brigades / Ethiopian forces / Planned Italian line / "Schematic · positions approximate") stays top right.
  - Extra hold time: plan +1.0 s, brigades +1.0 s, Albertone +0.8 s, gap +0.8 s, Dabormida +1.0 s, pieces +0.8 s, south map +0.6 s.
  - The "SATELLITE IMAGE, 2025 · MOVEMENTS SCHEMATIC, POSITIONS APPROXIMATE" tag is kept, so no frame implies an 1896 map.
