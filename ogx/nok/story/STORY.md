# OGX Nok — story-first plan (DRAFT v0.2, 30 Oct 2026)
Source of every claim: OGX Research — Nok evidence pack (ogx-nok-1, 30 Oct 2026), Eden 6015c5ee-f0fb-4748-a157-52aca5ee0015. Source keys are the pack's own (BR16, FR16, MB16, JU16, CH22, UR26, BRIT, ICOM, ATW11, CMA). Nothing here comes from outside the pack.

## (a) Central question
Could small, dispersed farming communities with no sign so far of a state, towns or an elite have made some of the earliest large-scale sculpture known from sub-Saharan Africa?

Answer the evidence allows, in two sentences: the Frankfurt excavators set out assuming great art needed a complex society, and after more than a decade they found small, autonomous farming groups and no evidence of a state or an elite, so they now describe Nok as complex "only in terms of ritual". That is a revised hypothesis, not proof: preservation is poor, the study area is limited, and fieldwork stopped in 2017.

## Creative differentiation note (v. Adwa and Timbuktu)
- Hook: a single object's face, not a picture-reading opener or event.
- Structure: an investigation that overturns its own starting hypothesis, not a battle or a rescue.
- Maps: one locator and one chronology build only.
- Ending: an unfinished verdict in the researcher's words, not a callback recap.

## Picture rule for AI stills
No AI images of Nok terracottas themselves (they could be mistaken for real, unprovenanced artefacts). No AI faces of named people (Fagg, Breunig, Rupp, Franke). AI stills show places, processes, hands, soil and light.
