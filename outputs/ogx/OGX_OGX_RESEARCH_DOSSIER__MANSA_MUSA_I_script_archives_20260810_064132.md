## Four things for your attention

**Three of these are new — not in the dossier.**

1. **A `verified = true` citation contradicts the verified person record.** `citations` holds *"Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari — Mansa Musa's Hajj Crashes Gold Prices"* (1349), marked `verified = true`, whose quote reads *"crashed Mediterranean gold prices for a decade, forcing European merchants to import gold from Africa."* That's a verified row carrying the exact framing `people/mansa-musa` calls exaggerated, plus an unsourced causal claim about European merchants. It's a worse problem than the low-confidence event duplicates, because it's gated as verified. I did not use it.

2. **The manuscript count conflicts.** `people/abdul-kader-haidara` says "over 377,000"; `events/timbuktu-manuscript-rescue-2012` says "approximately 350,000". I put both on screen rather than pick.

3. **The rescue is misdated inside its own record.** The event is dated `2012` but its description opens "In January–February 2013". Narration says 2012 for the occupation and leaves the evacuation undated.

4. **The famous quote stayed off screen.** Al-Umari's *"flood of his generosity"* line sits in a `verified = false` citation, so per your own gate it isn't in the script. It's the natural Q-block for Chapter Four — pull it from Levtzion & Hopkins pp. 269–273, verify the row, and it drops in cleanly after `Q("Mansa Musa, sultan of Takrur…")`.
