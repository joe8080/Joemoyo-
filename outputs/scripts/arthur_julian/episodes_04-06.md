# Day 2: Episodes 4–6, "The Rival" arc (canon, from Joe)

Replaces the earlier Ep 4–6 draft (shredder and CFO plot). Fitted to the **~30s per episode** format: one Seedance 2.5 generation, 210 Higgsfield credits.

**New character, Victor (the rival CEO):** 45, sharp features, an aggressive pinstripe suit, cold and calculating, wealthy but ruthless. His office is hyper-modern and minimalist, with black marble, stark white light and floor-to-ceiling windows over the city. Make one portrait once, then lock it.

**Fictional company:** "Ravencore Logistics" (Joe's draft said "Apex Logistics", which is a real company).

**Recurring hook:** Arthur's eyes glow or flash blue on his power lines.

## Episode 4 — The Rival's Move
1. **0–9s, Victor's office.** Low angle; he smiles sinisterly at a glowing tablet.
   VICTOR: "Julian is out. The old man is exposed. It's time to bleed them dry. Start the buyout."
2. **9–20s, warehouse aisle.** Victor strides in, flanked by two large security contractors; the workers look nervous.
   VICTOR: "Attention everyone! As of midnight, this facility belongs to Ravencore Logistics. Your current contracts are void."
3. **20–30s, cliffhanger.** Over Victor's shoulder: Arthur stands calmly at the end of the aisle, holding one sheet of paper.
   ARTHUR: "You're trespassing, Victor. And you clearly didn't read the fine print on the land deed."

## Episode 5 — The Loyalty Test
1. **0–10s.** Close-up: Victor drops a thick stack of cash on a packing crate in front of Marcus.
   VICTOR: "Walk away right now, Marcus. I'll triple your salary. The old man can't protect you."
2. **10–21s.** Marcus looks at the cash, then at Arthur in the background.
   MARCUS: "I don't work for a paycheck, Victor. I work for the man who built this place with his bare hands."
3. **21–30s, cliffhanger.** Extreme close-up: Victor, furious and humiliated, signals his guards forward.
   VICTOR: "Loyalty is a disease. Clear them out. By force if you have to."

## Episode 6 — The Checkmate
1. **0–10s.** Wide: the guards advance on Arthur and Marcus.
   VICTOR: "You're living in the past, Arthur. I own the supply chains. I own the politicians. You have nothing."
2. **10–20s.** Close-up: Arthur presses one red button on his phone; his eyes flash blue; a confident smirk.
   ARTHUR: "I don't need politicians. I own the global server grid you just tried to route your buyout through."
3. **20–30s, cliffhanger.** Victor's phone flashes red, "FUNDS FROZEN"; panic; the warehouse lights cut out except one spotlight on Arthur.
   ARTHUR: "Check your accounts, Victor. You just bought yourself a one-way ticket to bankruptcy."
