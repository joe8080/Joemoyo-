WHAT IS CONFIRMED, REPORTED AND UNRESOLVED
• Confirmed: Jensen Huang's staff email of 8 November; the board's independent investigation; the House chairs' request for a closed-door briefing.
• Reported: the New York Times' account of a US intelligence assessment; a proposed shareholder class action (Reuters); President Trump's remark that the FBI is "looking into it".
• Unresolved: whether any secrets or national security were compromised; what the board will find; whether the briefing takes place on 18 November.
• China's foreign ministry has called the spying claims a groundless accusation.
