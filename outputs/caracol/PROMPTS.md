# Caracol Reconstruction — Prompt Pack

Four jobs: one ancient Maya city (Caracol, Cayo District, Belize, c. AD 650), shown four ways.
Generated with Artlist (Nano Banana 2, 16:9, 2K). Saved in Artlist under Default Project → "Caracol Reconstruction".

## Facts the prompts are built on
- **Caana ("Sky Place")**: 43 m acropolis, still the tallest building in Belize. Three summit temples plus a royal palace courtyard.
- **Epicentre**: Plaza B at Caana's foot; Plaza A with an E-Group (solstice observatory) and Structure A6, the Temple of the Wooden Lintel; two ball courts; stelae and altars.
- **Scale**: about 200 km² of settlement mapped by LiDAR in 2009 (Chase & Chase, Caracol Archaeological Project); estimated 100,000+ people at its peak.
- **Landscape**: raised plastered causeways (sacbeob) radiating up to ~10 km; tens of thousands of contour terraces; rainwater reservoirs; courtyard house groups.
- **History**: Caracol defeated Tikal in AD 562 (recorded on Altar 21).

Note: Caracol is among the largest Maya cities **by area**. Tikal, Calakmul and El Mirador compete on other measures.

3. For a YouTube thumbnail, crop `03` tight on Caana and add a 3-word title.
4. Label it "artist's reconstruction" on screen: it's education-first and policy-safe.
