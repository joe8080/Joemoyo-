Pricing these properly

I could not look up a single price — this machine's network blocks every card-price source, and a guessed price on a card you're about to sell is worse than no price.

  1. eBay sold listings — filter to Sold items, not active. Active asking prices are fiction; sold is what someone actually paid.
  2. Cardmarket (best for UK/EU) — use Trend price, not the lowest listing. The lowest is usually damaged or overseas.
  3. TCGplayer Market Price — US-centric, so convert and expect a gap.
  4. Price by exact variant. Trick or Trade cards carry a Halloween stamp; a stamped card and its base-set twin are different products and can differ a lot.
  5. Condition drives the number. NM to LP is often a 20–40% drop on modern cards.

Enter what you find in each card's price field. The board records the source and date, and marks anything older than 30 days as stale.
