# BRIEF

workflow: general-video
flow: standard

## Message

One operator, five ventures, one system — JoeMoyo builds media, markets, and
machines that run themselves.

## Deliverable

- **Format:** 1920×1080, 16:9, 30fps
- **Duration:** 240s (4:00)
- **Audio:** music bed, no narration (VO can be added later)
- **Destination:** portfolio / website hero / YouTube channel trailer

## Audience

Prospective collaborators, clients, and partners evaluating Joe's range across
media production, finance content, music, commerce, and AI automation.

## Visual direction

Locked OGX prestige palette — gold on near-black. Reads as documentary, not
startup deck.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| GOLD | `#C9A84C` | labels, rim light, accents, rules |
| DARK | `#1A1A2E` | near-black ground |
| CRIMSON | `#8B1A1A` | commerce colour-pop |
| AMBER | `#FFF3CD` | music / warmth colour-pop |
| TEAL | `#0F4C75` | systems / finance colour-pop |
| WHITE | `#FFFFFF` | hook text |

One controlled colour-pop per act. Never more than one.

## Production method

Hero stills generated in Higgsfield; motion, typography, transitions and
assembly authored in HyperFrames. No AI-generated on-screen text — all type is
rendered by the composition so it stays crisp and correct.

## Structure

| Act | Window | Subject | Pop |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0–18s | Cold open — title | gold only |
| 1 | 18–68s | OrigineX Human Archives (history YouTube) | amber |
| 2 | 68–115s | Finance channel + AI Finance Toolkit | teal |
| 3 | 115–155s | Music studio | amber |
| 4 | 155–190s | Shopify commerce | crimson |
| 5 | 190–222s | Agent systems + algorithmic trading | teal |
| 6 | 222–240s | Close / lockup | gold only |

## Constraints

- **No fabricated metrics.** No subscriber counts, revenue figures, or results
  claims unless supplied by Joe. Copy describes what each venture *is*.
- No real faces presented as Joe or as clients.
- Motion obeys `motion-doctrine`: the film reads as one continuous camera move,
  no idle wobble, seams carry velocity.
